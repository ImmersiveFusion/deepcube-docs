# DeepCube™ 1.26.1

Everything since 1.26.0 lands in this Steam update. On a healthy grid, Mosey now tours your services instead of standing still. If a grid refuses you entry, DeepCube now says so instead of leaving you in an empty grid.

## What's New
- Mosey tours a healthy grid: when Mosey roams a grid where nothing is failing, erroring or slow, he now visits every service, starting with the services that send their own telemetry and then the busiest, and pauses for three seconds at each one, facing it. Before, he stood still. Trouble still comes first: if a service starts failing during the tour, he leaves the tour for it at his next stop.

## Fixes
- Fixed entering a grid you can no longer open leaving you in an empty grid. If the grid is no longer available to you, DeepCube now says "This grid is no longer available to you." and reopens the grid picker. If your sign-in has expired, it says "Your sign-in has expired. Sign in again to open this grid." and takes you to sign in.

## Known Issues
- A dependency's numbers and error state can differ between DeepCube and DeepCube Web.
- Demo mode hides only the grid's name. Hosts, URLs and database names are shown as your services report them. Check what is visible before you demo or share your screen.
- macOS support is experimental and may lack full feature parity with Windows.
