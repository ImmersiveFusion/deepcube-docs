# IAPM 1.18.0

Ask Tessa anything and she now answers in a dedicated in-world chat window, with proper formatting, streaming replies, and cards for the tools she runs. The service graph also runs smoother in busy scenes. Everything since 1.17.0 lands in this Steam update.

## What's New
- In-world chat window: Tessa now answers in a dedicated in-world panel. Open it, type your question, and the answer streams back with formatted text, code highlighting, and tool-call cards. The companion menu options open here too. The panel centers when it opens, dims when you are not using it, and can be hidden or closed; while you type, your input stays in the chat instead of driving the game. Tessa follows your current workspace and persona.
- Steadier service graph: service pulses and falling blocks now appear only when they are on screen and are paced to your frame rate, so busy fleets stay smoother.

## Known Issues
- The chat window handles one conversation at a time, and there is no saved history yet, so closing it starts a fresh conversation.
- macOS support is experimental and may lack full feature parity with Windows.
