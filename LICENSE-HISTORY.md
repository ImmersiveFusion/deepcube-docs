has a two-line answer rather than requiring git archaeology.

## Why the window matters, and how little

A Creative Commons licence is irrevocable as to its licensees. Relicensing is a
unilateral act and needed nobody's consent, but anyone who obtained the content
during that window holds a perpetual CC BY 4.0 grant **to the versions published
in it**. Everything from `ac1613b` onward is Apache-2.0 only.

Given the length of the window, the practical residue is nil. It is recorded
because that is cheap now and expensive to reconstruct later.
