# Uninstalling on Windows

<!-- SP-074: the Add/Remove Programs entries below still say the currently-shipping name.

     string until the code ships the new one. Do not "fix" these to

1. Open the Control Panel on your computer.
1. Click on the `Programs` or `Programs and Features` option.
1. Find `Immersive APM` in the list of applications.
1. Click `Immersive APM` then click the `Uninstall` button.
1. Follow the instructions provided by the uninstallation wizard to complete the process.
1. All set!
